Team Structure

Our diverse team is organized into specialized subteams, each contributing unique expertise to our solar car project.

ARRAY

Array

Design and optimize the solar panel systems for maximum energy capture.

BATT

Battery

Develop and manage the energy storage systems that power our solar car.

BIZ

Business

Handle sponsorships, outreach, and the business side of our operations.

ELEC

Electrical

Develop the electrical systems, wiring, and power management.

MECH

Mechanical

Design and build the mechanical systems, aerodynamic profile, and composite structures of the car.

OPS

Operations

Coordinate logistics, planning, and day-to-day team operations.

CODE

Software

Develop software for data logging, telemetry, car control systems, and race strategy optimization.
